          Help

          How to use Synergy

          1. Start of the day: Synergy asks how you feel and can add starter tasks for beginners. You can edit them before adding.
          2. Timer: tap the time to change its length. Mood, stats and today's tasks are around the timer. Tabs switch to Alarm, Stopwatch and Clock.
          3. When a session ends: a full-screen reminder asks if you want to take a break or keep studying. Breaks suggest stretching, no phone, recalling what you learned and an eye rest.
          4. Calendar: Today, Schedule, Week, Month or Year. Open a day to add tasks with +, stickers and your mood.
          5. Chat: message yourself. Use + to attach, the clock button to set when it arrives, and the Files tab for everything you saved.
          6. Dictionary: the book button at the top looks up a word and keeps a list of your saved words. It also works while the timer runs.
          7. Strict mode: after you type a promise you cannot pause, reset or skip. Notes, the dictionary and saved study items still work.
          8. Videos: YouTube links you saved open in a focus player. You are warned if another video opens or you leave the page.
          9. Alarms and reminders: they ring full screen while the page is open. Set a phone alarm too for important ones.
